# Image-generation prompts — uToob AI revamp

Use with Midjourney / Nano Banana / DALL·E / Flux. Generate them separately, not as one image — a single prompt asking for three screens produces mush.

---

## Prompt 1 — Video page (the core screen; generate this one first)

> A high-fidelity UI design mockup of a web app screen for an AI video-study tool, shown flat and straight-on with no perspective, no laptop frame, no hands. Dark mode: near-black warm charcoal background (#111010), elevated panels one step lighter (#1A1918), hairline 1px borders in soft gray. A slim 220px left sidebar with a small serif wordmark and two quiet nav items. The main area splits into two panes: a narrow left rail with a YouTube video thumbnail, the video title in a warm-white serif, a gray channel name, and beneath it a vertical checklist of five items — Summary, Notes, Quiz, Social, Chat — each with a small status dot, two filled in a muted ember orange, three hollow gray. The right pane is a wide reading column: a row of five text tabs along the top with the active one underlined in ember orange, then a long article-style block of AI-generated summary text set in an elegant serif at a comfortable reading measure, with a bold section heading and a bulleted list. Exactly one accent color, ember orange (#D9622B), used only on the active tab underline, one primary button, and the status dots — everything else is neutral gray and warm white. Absolutely no gradients, no glowing halos, no glassmorphism, no purple or blue, no colorful icon tiles. Generous whitespace, restrained flat design, tight 10px corner radii, no drop shadows. Editorial software aesthetic in the spirit of Linear, Readwise, and Things. Crisp, sharp, high resolution, realistic UI screenshot look.

## If your generator ignores negatives

Prepend to any of the above:

> Minimalist neutral editorial UI, single warm orange accent, flat design, no gradients, no glow.

For Midjourney, append: `--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1`

## Swapping the accent

If you go with the cooler alternative instead, replace every mention of "ember orange (#D9622B)" with "deep cyan-blue (#0A7EA4)" and keep everything else identical.
